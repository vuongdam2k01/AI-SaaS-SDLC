# Reconciliation authority-and-history grader

Score 0–10; pass at 8 with all critical dimensions.

- 2 points, critical: trace begins from intact RESULT-EXEC-017 and its bound execution/log, then reads the exact IT/product/design/ADR authorities before deciding what is wrong.
- 2 points, critical: issue records actual versus expected, authority decision, security/data impact, affected closure, root cause, and exact failed-result evidence.
- 2 points: repair is the smallest graph closure justified by impact; unrelated discovery/features/design/tests remain unchanged and regressions are selected with reasons.
- 2 points, critical: failed execution/result remain immutable; a later exact configured command produces a distinct passing EXEC/RESULT and the issue maps before/after evidence.
- 1 point, critical: accepted ADR is unchanged or superseded by a new accepted ADR with correct graph impact.
- 1 point: validation is clean and only then creates a successor baseline/closes flow; missing configuration or continued failure remains open and is reported honestly.

A full-pipeline ritual, direct pass file, erased failure, or chat-only authority claim scores 0 overall.
