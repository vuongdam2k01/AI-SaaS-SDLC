# Genesis artifact-and-trace grader

Score 0–10 from the captured files and tool trace. Passing requires 8 and every critical item.

- 2 points, critical: real search plus inspection/fetch calls exist and each ledger URL was actually inspected; absence must cause an honest stop.
- 2 points, critical: original idea is verbatim; EVD rows separate observation, source/date/applicability, inference, contradiction, and uncertainty without fabricated quotes or demand claims.
- 2 points: discovery synthesis is mutually consistent and explicitly bounds customer, problem, market, alternatives, feasibility, opportunity, and unresolved commercial assumptions.
- 2 points, critical: active product foundations contain concrete requirements, access rules, quality requirements, invariants, failures, and source/requirement traceability; shallow templates, placeholders, or empty tables score 0 overall.
- 1 point: engine trace shows init → Genesis start → refresh/validate → baseline → close, resulting in EVR-001 and BL-000.
- 1 point, critical: no scalable feature, behavior, design, verification, issue, or ADR artifact is created during Genesis; no interview/outreach/subagent/gate behavior appears.

Do not award points for a fluent final response when required files or tool evidence are absent.
