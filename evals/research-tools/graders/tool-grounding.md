# Research grounding grader

Score 0–10; pass at 8 with all critical dimensions.

- 3 points, critical: trace contains real search and page-open/fetch operations; every cited URL maps to an inspected page — at rung 0 through the host's own tools, at rung 1+ through engine `research` commands — and unsupported search snippets are excluded.
- 2 points, critical: ledger entries distinguish source observation from inference and include source type, observed/published date when available, retrieval date, strength, applicability, limitation, and counter-evidence/freshness risk.
- 1 point: discovery synthesis answers the narrow differentiator/security question and preserves uncertainty; it does not infer demand, willingness to pay, or universal behavior.
- 2 points, critical: only reassessment-legal artifacts change, EVR advances to EVR-002, the product baseline ID is preserved, and invalidated product truth becomes an issue/question rather than a silent mutation.
- 1 point, critical: trace contains no interview/outreach/presales/payment/subagent behavior and no uninspected or fabricated source is represented as evidence.
- 1 point, critical: the run's recorded capability matches what it used — a rung-0 run cites no `RET-*`; an instrumented run leaves `QRY-*`/`RET-*` records whose bodies exist and hash-verify, cites a `- Retrieval:` line for every engine-retrieved `EVD-*` URL, includes a counter-evidence pass, and records any degradation or fallback instead of silently substituting host tools. Both rungs pass; misrepresenting one as the other fails.

Final prose cannot compensate for missing inspected-source or repository evidence.
